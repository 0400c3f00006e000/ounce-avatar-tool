# Ounce Avatar Tool

## I AM NOT RESPONSIBLE IF YOU BRICK YOUR CONSOLE!

Simple tool to change the avatar of a profile on your Switch 2 to a JPG from your SD card.

> [!NOTE]
> Your icon may not show up properly in all places until you reboot your Switch 2. Additionally it will not show up in certain places including the user page but I do plan on fixing this at some point.

## Usage

Copy the NRO to `sdmc:/switch` and place a 256x256 JPG in `sdmc:/avatars`. Launch the tool, select your image and then the profile you would like to apply the image to.

## Building

Get [ounce-devkit](https://github.com/ouncebrew/ounce-devkit), then run `make`
